Foundation

Layout

Five sizes from extra small to extra large, a page that is one column until 992px, and two screens that fill the window and never scroll.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Breakpoints
  • Live grid
  • Screen templates
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Every screen is designed for a phone held upright, a tablet, a laptop and a large desktop. Menus are one column that becomes two at 992px. Duel and World Map are exactly as tall as the window.

Usage

  • Build every screen for every width at once. No screen is made for one size and patched for the rest.
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at 320px, or at 200% zoom.
  • Every control is at least 44 × 44px.
  • On Duel the flash card is the largest thing at every width.
