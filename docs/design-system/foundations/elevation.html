Foundation

Elevation

Things rise in two ways: a soft shadow for what floats, and a solid ledge for what you can press.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The game looks like toys on a table. A button sits on a solid base and sinks when pressed. A panel over the classroom sits on a solid ledge. Soft shadows are kept for what floats above everything: toasts and dialogs.

Usage

  • If it can be pressed, it sits on a base. If it only informs, it is flat with a soft edge.
  • In dark, shadows are darker and raised surfaces are lighter.
