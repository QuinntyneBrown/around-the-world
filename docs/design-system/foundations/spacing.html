Spacing

A 4px scale from 0 to 96px. Inside a component use the small steps; between parts of a screen use the large ones.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • The scale
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Every gap and padding is a step of the scale. Things that belong together sit 8 to 12px apart; a panel pads 20 to 24px; parts of a screen sit 24 to 32px apart.
