Foundation

Motion

The hop is the reward and lasts about a second. Everything else is quick, and losing is gentle.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • Choreography
  • Reduced motion

Overview

Motion says what just happened: a card flips in, a kid hops on, a button sinks. Nothing plays for show, and nothing punishes: no red screen, no buzzer, no shake that lasts.

Reduced motion

When the device asks for reduced motion, the duration tokens drop to nothing and the hop becomes a fade of 200 ms. A card flip, a shake, a stamp landing and the Podium rising become the same quick fade, --duration-fade, which keeps its 200 ms when the other durations drop. Nothing moves across the screen: no hop, no flip, no shake, no tilt on stamps and stickers, no listening rings. The timer bar still drains, because it is a clock.

Usage

  • Use a duration token; never a number.
  • Animate transform and opacity only.
  • Nothing animates when a screen opens except skeletons.
  • A sound never plays before the child has touched the page, and every sound can be muted.
