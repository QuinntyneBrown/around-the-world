Overview

These are the system’s commitments. Each component page says how it keeps them under its own Accessibility heading.

Perceivable

CommitmentHow
Text contrast 4.5 to 1; large text and control parts 3 to 1Pairs in contrast-pairs.json, checked in both themes
Colour is never the only carrierA chosen thing has a tick or dot; a kid has a name; frozen and lost are said in words
ReflowNo sideways scroll at 320px or at 200% zoom
ImagesPortraits beside a name are decorative; alone they are named. The classroom is decorative; the World Map is described
MotionReduced motion replaces hops, flips and shakes with fades
SoundEvery sound can be muted; nothing plays before the child touches the page

Operable

CommitmentHow
KeyboardEvery control is reached with Tab and worked with Enter or Space. On Duel, digits, Backspace and Enter work the number pad
Focus visibleOne ring: 4px, 3px offset, on :focus-visible
Focus orderThe order on the screen. Dialogs hold focus and return it
Target size44 × 44px for every control
TimingThe card’s time is the game, and Chill gives 10 seconds. A solo game can be paused. Toasts pause while focused; failures stay
Skip link“Skip to the game” on every screen

Understandable

CommitmentHow
Words a ten-year-old usesSee Content
Errors say what to do“Hmm, no room with that code. Check the letters?”
Nothing to remember or typeNo account, no password, no name. The one field is four letters read aloud
ConsistentThe same words for the same things on every screen

Robust

CommitmentHow
Name, role, valueNative buttons and links first. Toggles use aria-pressed; the switch uses role="switch"; the dialog follows the WAI-ARIA modal dialog pattern
Status messagesA new card, a result and the freeze go to an assertive live region; joins and stickers to a polite one; failures are alerts

Testing

  • Play a whole solo tour with a keyboard alone, and again with a screen reader.
  • Run the contrast check after any change to a colour.
  • Check every new screen at 320px and at 200% zoom.
  • Turn on reduced motion and play a duel won and a duel lost.
