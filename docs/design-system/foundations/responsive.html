Responsive

Every screen works from the narrowest phone to the widest desktop, and Duel fills the screen at all of them.

Overview

Styles start at 320px and add columns as the window grows. Width decides the layout; height matters on the two screens that never scroll.

Short screens

A phone turned on its side is about 390px tall. Duel still fills it without scrolling: under 672px tall the card and keys shrink, and under 544px tall the world ring gives way to “Desk 5 of 12” in the HUD. The flash card and the pad are both whole.

Touch and pointer

  • Every target is at least 44 × 44px; number pad keys never go below 44px tall.
  • Nothing depends on hover.
  • Dialogs are sheets from the bottom edge under 576px.

The mocks were checked for sideways scroll at 360, 768 and 1280px in both themes, and Duel for any scroll at 320 × 568, 360 × 640 and 844 × 390.

Usage

  • Write the phone layout first and add to it with min-width queries.
  • Use the four breakpoints; a component may add a height query when it lives on Duel.
