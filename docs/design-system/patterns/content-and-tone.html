Content and tone

How the game speaks at each moment of a tour.

Overview

The full rules are in Content. This page shows them at work across one game.

When to use what

MomentToneWords
OpeningInviting“Shout the answer first. Hop to the next desk. Make it all the way round.”
ChoosingPlain“Which maths?” · “How far round?”
SafetyReassuring, with a reason“Your voice is never saved or sent to anyone.”
A right answerGlad, short“= 24 You got it!”
A wrong answerLight“Not 42! Frozen for a sec…”
Losing a duelGentle“Nia said 24 first. So close!”
WaitingCalm“Hang tight! Your spot at Italy is saved for 30 seconds.”
The endProud for everyone“You went around the world!” · “Nia made it round first!”

States

StateRule
Good newsOne exclamation mark
Bad newsNo exclamation mark, no capitals, a way on
Anything a model wroteNever shown

Responsive behaviour

Words are the same at every width. A phone may drop a label to its icon only where the icon button keeps the spoken name.

Accessibility

  • Plain words are the first accessibility feature: short sentences, no idioms that need explaining, numbers as figures.
  • Screen reader text says the same as the screen, not more.

Content

  • Read every new line aloud. If a ten-year-old would not say it, change it.
