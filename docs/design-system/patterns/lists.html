Pattern

Lists: players, standings and stamps

The three collections in the game. None is longer than 24.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • When to use what
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don’t
  • Sources

Overview

There are no tables. A room has up to 8 players, the standings have 5 kids, and a passport has 6, 12 or 24 stamps. Each is a list with a fixed size, so nothing grows without end.

When to use what

CollectionUseSize
Players in a roomPlayer cards in a gridAlways 8 places: players and empty desks
How far each kid has travelledStandingsAlways 5
Countries reachedPassport stampsThe tour length
The top of a finished tourPodium3, or 2 with two players
The game’s setupSummary3 lines

Structure

  1. 1Kenji5/12
  2. 2You4/12
  3. 3Nia3/12
  4. 4Milo2/12
  5. 5Pip1/12
Standings: an ordered list with the child marked in words

States

StateRule
LoadingSkeletons in the shape of the items
A place not filledShown as a place: an empty desk, a “?” stamp
YouMarked “You” or “(you)”, with an edge as well
Two the sameTwo players as one kid are told apart by nickname

Responsive behaviour

Player cards go 2, 3 then 4 across. Standings are five columns on a phone and one row above. Stamps go 3 or 4 across, 6 for a long tour.

Accessibility

  • Real lists: <ul> for players and stamps, <ol> for standings and the podium.
  • The players list is a polite live region.
  • Order for a screen reader is the true order (1st, 2nd, 3rd) even when drawn differently.

Content

  • Counts as “4/8” with a spoken “4 of 8 players”.
