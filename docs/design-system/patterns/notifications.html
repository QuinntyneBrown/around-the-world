Notifications

The four kinds of passing message, and which one a moment gets.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility

Overview

The game interrupts as little as it can. During a duel nothing appears over the flash card.

When to use what

MessageWhereStays
ToastLobby: a friend joined or left, link copied, you are the host now4 seconds; a failure stays
Map messageWorld Map: who walks on, who sits down1.6 seconds
Sticker popDuel: a cheer from a watching playerAbout 2 seconds
Alert and banner as a bannerDuel and Solo Or Friends: the connectionUntil it is no longer true

Structure

Brave Narwhal joinedas Pip

You walk on to USA!

A toast and a map message

States

StateRule
Several at onceThree toasts at most, newest at the bottom; one map message at a time
Pointed at or focusedA toast’s timer pauses
A failureNever leaves by itself

Responsive behaviour

Toasts span the bottom on a phone and sit bottom right from 576px. Map messages are centred under the header. Stickers arrive at the right edge above the footer.

Accessibility

  • Each kind has its own live region, present in the page even when empty.
  • Nothing steals focus.
  • No sound that is not also shown.

Content

  • Three or four words for the title. A sticker’s words are fixed.
