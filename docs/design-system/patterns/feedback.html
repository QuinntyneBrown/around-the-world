Pattern

Feedback and loading

Which kind of answer the game gives to what just happened, and what shows while something arrives.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • When to use what
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don’t
  • Sources

Overview

Everything a child does gets an answer within a tenth of a second, in the place they are looking.

When to use what

What happenedWhere the answer goes
A key or button was pressedThe control sinks; a busy button says “Joining…”
A duel was decidedOn the Flash card, in words, and through the live region
A hopStamp card, then the World Map with a Map message
Something about what you just did failedAn Alert and banner or Field message beside the control
Something happened in the roomA Toast
The whole game cannot go onA Status overlay
A screen is arrivingSkeletons in its shape
A button’s work is under wayA Spinner in the button

Structure

6 × 4

= 24 You got it!

The answer is where the child is looking

States

StateRule
Under 300 msShow nothing
A screen arrivingSkeletons; the way on is usable first
A classmate’s turnNever a wait: the card is never held for the on-device model
The classroom failed to drawA still picture stands in and the game plays

Responsive behaviour

Alerts fill their column. Toasts sit at the bottom on a phone and bottom right from 576px. On Duel, banners go across the top and nothing covers the flash card.

Accessibility

  • A new card, a result and the freeze: aria-live="assertive".
  • Joins, stickers, map messages: polite.
  • Failures: role="alert", and they stay until dealt with.
  • A loading container has aria-busy="true" and a hidden status in words.

Content

  • Good news is short and glad. Bad news says what to do. Losing is gentle.
