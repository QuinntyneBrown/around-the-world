Panel

A white surface with a soft edge that groups one decision: the nickname, the game’s setup, a kind of choice.

Overview

Use a panel to hold one thing a child decides or reads. Do not nest panels.

Anatomy

  1. SurfaceWhite, 32px radius
  2. Edge2px, inside
  3. Titleh2, optional
  4. Content20px padding, 24px from 576px

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> named by its heading with aria-labelledby.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

The heading names it.

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • The title is a question or a noun: “Which maths?”, “The game”.
