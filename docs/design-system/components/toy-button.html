Toy button

The chunky button that sits on a solid base and sinks when pressed. Each screen has one big one: the way on.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .toy-btn

Overview

Use a toy button for the action a screen exists for (Play, I’m Zuri!, Start the race, Go!) and for the few other real actions beside it. Use a text button for a quiet alternative and an icon button for a corner control.

  • Play (red) is the one way on. One per screen.
  • Friends (blue) makes or joins a room.
  • Solo (yellow, navy label) plays alone.
  • Go (green) turns the mic on.
  • Paper (white) is the second choice beside a coloured one.

Anatomy

  1. Icon (optional)24px, the label’s colour
  2. LabelFredoka semibold, a verb
  3. Face56px tall, 16px radius
  4. Base6px solid ledge under the face

Responsive behaviour

WidthWhat changes
Under 576pxThe extra large button is 64px tall and fills the width. “New name” shows its icon only.
576px and upThe extra large button is 80px tall with 30px type; on Home it hugs its label.
992px and upIn a two-column screen it sits at the bottom of the second column.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button">, or an <a> when it only goes to another screen. Busy sets aria-busy="true" and keeps the width.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves focus to the button.
EnterPresses it.
SpacePresses it.

Focus

The shared ring: 4px of --color-focus-ring, 3px away from the edge, on :focus-visible.

Labelling

The visible label is the name. An icon is decorative (aria-hidden).

Motion

Pressing sinks the face onto its base in 120 ms (--duration-fast). With reduced motion it does not move.

Touch

48px tall at the smallest, 44px wide at least.

Content

  • A verb a ten-year-old says: “Play”, “Join”, “Go!”. Never “Submit” or “OK”.
  • Two or three words at most, so it fits a phone.
  • Busy says what is happening: “Joining…”, “Saving…”.
