Player card

One desk in the Lobby: a player’s kid and nickname, or an empty desk.

Overview

Eight cards show the room filling up. Two players may pick the same kid; their nicknames tell them apart.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list item in a <ul aria-live="polite">, so a player who joins is announced.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

The nickname and “as {kid}” are text; the picture is decorative.

Contrast

Motion

A new player fades and rises in over 200 ms; reduced motion only fades.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • Nickname first, then “as {kid}”.
  • “(you)” in words, and “Host” in words.
  • An empty desk says “Empty desk”.
