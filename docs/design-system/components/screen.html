Screen and stack

The frame every screen sits in, and the two helpers that space what is inside it.

Overview

.screen is a column at least as tall as the window. .screen--fill is exactly as tall as the window and never scrolls: Duel and World Map. .screen__body centres the content and becomes two columns from 992px. .stack spaces things down a column and .cluster along a row.

Anatomy

  1. Top barOptional
  2. BodyUp to 1152px wide, page margins from the layout tokens
  3. ColumnsOne, then two from 992px

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The screen holds one <header> (when there is a top bar) and one <main id="main">.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

Focus follows the order on the screen: first column, then second.

Labelling

Sections inside are named with aria-labelledby pointing at their heading.

Contrast

Text on the page
Text on the page

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • One h1 per screen; panels use h2.
