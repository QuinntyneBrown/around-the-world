Switch

A setting that is on or off at once: Sound, and Answer by shouting.

Overview

Use a switch only in Settings, where a change takes effect the moment it is made and is kept on the device.

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe setting’s name
  2. HelpWhat off means
  3. Track64 × 44px
  4. ThumbSlides; the track says On or Off

Variants

It has one look.

Sizes

One size.

States

State ofDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Off
On

Both themes

SoundPops, chimes and jingles.
SoundPops, chimes and jingles.

Responsive behaviour

It is the same at every width.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

SoundPops, chimes and jingles.
SoundPops, chimes and jingles.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <button role="switch" aria-checked>, named by the setting’s label and described by its help.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the switch.
Space or EnterTurns it on or off.

Focus

The shared ring: 4px of --color-focus-ring, 3px away from the edge, on :focus-visible.

Labelling

The label names the setting, not the action: “Sound”, not “Turn sound off”.

Contrast

On, text
Off, edge
On, text
Off, edge

Motion

The thumb slides in 120 ms; with reduced motion it jumps.

Touch

64 × 44px.

Content

  • The track says “On” or “Off” in words.
  • Help says what off means: “Off means you tap answers on the number pad.”
