Mode card

One of the two ways to play on Solo Or Friends: Just me, or With friends.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .mode-grid

Overview

A panel with an icon, a title, one sentence, a picture of what you get, and the action at the bottom.

Anatomy

  1. Icon48px tile
  2. Titleh2
  3. SentenceOne
  4. ActionsAt the bottom, full width

Variants

Just me

Race the class. Your classmates are played by the computer.

With friends

Play live with up to 8 friends. Share a 4-letter code to get them in.

Responsive behaviour

WidthWhat changes
Under 576pxOne card under the other; the two friend actions stack.
576px and upThe two friend actions sit side by side wherever the card has room for both (each at least 160px), and stack where it does not, so neither sticks out of the card.
768px and upThe two cards sit side by side.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.
