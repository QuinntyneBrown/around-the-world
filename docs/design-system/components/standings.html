Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <ol> labelled “Standings, by desks travelled”. The child’s item has aria-current="true".

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

Place has aria-label “Place 2”; the distance reads “4 of 12 desks”.

Contrast

Text on a panel
Secondary text on a panel
Your edge
Text on a panel
Secondary text on a panel
Your edge

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • “You”, never the child’s kid’s name.
  • Distance as “4/12” in tabular figures.
