World ring

A tiny map of every desk round the room, with a dot for each kid, on Duel.

Overview

It shows how far round everyone is at a glance. Under 768px it gives way to a line of text.

Anatomy

  1. TrackA ring
  2. DesksA dot each; the start is navy
  3. KidsA dot in each kid’s colour
  4. YouLarger, with a navy edge
  5. Label“Desk 5 of 12”

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Desk 5 of 12

Desk 5 of 12

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="img" with a label that says the tour length and the child’s desk.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

The label under it repeats the desk in text.

Contrast

Your dot’s edge
Your dot’s edge

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • “Desk {n} of {tour length}”.
