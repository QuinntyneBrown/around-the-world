Top bar

The slim header on every menu screen: Back, the screen’s title, and an optional right slot.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .topbar

Overview

It carries the screen’s one <h1>. Home, Duel, World Map and Podium have no top bar: they fill the screen their own way.

Anatomy

  1. BackAn icon button
  2. TitleThe screen’s h1, centred
  3. Right slot48px wide, usually empty, keeps the title centred

Variants

Who’s playing?

With Back
HTML
<header class="topbar"><a class="icon-btn" href="#top" aria-label="Back"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true">…</svg></a><p class="topbar__title">Who’s playing?</p><div class="topbar__slot"></div></header>

Mic check

Without Back
HTML
<header class="topbar"><span class="topbar__slot"></span><p class="topbar__title">Mic check</p><div class="topbar__slot"></div></header>

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header> with the page’s <h1>. Back is a link to the screen before.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack is the first control after the skip link.
EnterGoes back, with what was chosen still chosen.

Focus

The shared ring: 4px of --color-focus-ring, 3px away from the edge, on :focus-visible.

Labelling

Back’s aria-label is “Back”, or says what leaving does: “Leave the room”.

Contrast

Text on the page

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

Back is 48 × 48px.

Content

  • The title names the screen in the child’s words: “Pick your kid”, “Who’s playing?”, “Join a room”.
  • A long title is cut with an ellipsis; keep it to four words.
