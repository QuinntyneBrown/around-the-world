Toast

A short message in the corner of the Lobby about something that just happened: a friend joined, the link was copied.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .toast-region

Overview

Use it for an event that needs no decision. It leaves by itself, except when something failed.

Anatomy

  1. IconMatches the tone
  2. TitleWhat happened
  3. TextOne short line, optional
  4. Dismiss44px

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

In a region labelled “Messages” with aria-live="polite". A failure uses role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and Dismiss.
EnterPresses them.

Focus

The ring is white on the navy toast.

Labelling

Dismiss is named “Dismiss”.

Contrast

Text
Text

Motion

Rises in over 200 ms. Leaves after 4 seconds, pausing while pointed at or focused. A failure stays until dismissed.

Touch

Dismiss is 44 × 44px.

Content

  • Title in three words: “Link copied”.
  • A failure says what to do instead.
