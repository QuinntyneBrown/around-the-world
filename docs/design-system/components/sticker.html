Sticker

The six cheers a watching player can send, and how one shows when it arrives. There is no other way to say anything.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .sticker-bar

Overview

A sticker is one of six ids, never text. A player watching a duel has the bar; everyone sees a sent sticker with the sender’s kid for about 2 seconds.

Anatomy

  1. Sticker barSix buttons, only while watching
  2. StickerA kid’s face and two words at most
  3. Sticker popWhat everyone sees, with who sent it

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A role="group" labelled “Send a sticker” of native buttons. Arriving stickers go into a polite live region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves focus to the button.
EnterPresses it.
SpacePresses it.

Focus

The shared ring: 4px of --color-focus-ring, 3px away from the edge, on :focus-visible.

Labelling

The words on the sticker; the face is decorative. A pop adds who sent it.

Motion

A sticker pops in over 200 ms, stays about 2 seconds and fades. With reduced motion it is upright and only fades.

Touch

At least 44 × 44 px at every width (--target-min). One sticker a second at most; extra presses are dropped quietly.

Content

  • Two words at most, all kind: Go!, Wow!, Nice one, So close, Whoosh!, Beep boop!
  • Nothing a child can type, ever.
