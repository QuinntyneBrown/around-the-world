Status overlay

A card over a dimmed Duel while the game cannot go on: Reconnecting, or the game is lost.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableBlock .status-overlay

Overview

Use it only when the child can do nothing but wait, or when the only way on is Home. It is not a dialog: there is nothing to decide.

Anatomy

  1. ScrimOver the whole screen
  2. Icon40px
  3. TitleWhat is happening
  4. TextWhat it means for the child
  5. ActionOnly when waiting is over

Variants

Reconnecting…

Hang tight! Your spot at Italy is saved for 30 seconds.

We lost the game

The connection was away for too long, so your desk was given up. Nothing was your fault.

Responsive behaviour

Centred at every width, with 24px around it.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Content

  • Say what is saved: “Your spot at Italy is saved for 30 seconds.”
  • Say it was not the child’s fault.
