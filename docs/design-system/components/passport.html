Passport

The record of countries reached: a stamp for each, a placeholder for the rest, and the child’s place.

Overview

Every child gets a passport whatever their place. It is what makes a second tour worth playing.

  2. Count and place“9 of 12 countries · 2nd place”
  3. StampDashed edge, tilted

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list of stamps labelled “Stamps”.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

A missing stamp reads “Not reached”; the question mark is hidden.

Contrast

Text and stamps
Text and stamps

Motion

Stamps do not move. With reduced motion they are upright.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • “{n} of {tour length} countries · {place} place”.
