Responsive behaviour

576px and upNames 24px, portraits 56px.
Under 544px tallA phone on its side: “Desk 5 of 12” joins the row and the world ring goes.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text and a progress bar. Results are announced by Duel’s live region, not by the HUD.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

Each side reads name, then role. The role says who is who, so the side and the colour are not needed.

Contrast

Text on a panel
Secondary text on a panel
Text on a panel
Secondary text on a panel

Motion

It has no motion of its own.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • “You” for the child; a kid’s name for a classmate; a nickname for a friend.
  • Roles: “Travelling” and “Holds {country}”.
