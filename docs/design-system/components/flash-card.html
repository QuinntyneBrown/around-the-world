Flash card

The sum, and under it how to answer or what just happened. The largest thing on the Duel screen.

Overview

The card holds the sum in the biggest type in the game. Its foot changes with the duel: “Shout it!”, the answer box, the result, or a gentle loss.

Anatomy

  1. SumFredoka bold, 72 to 128px
  2. FootShout it!, the answer box, or the result
  3. Result ring6px, green or red, with words

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own. To restyle it, override its component custom properties or the semantic tokens listed under Tokens; never write a colour into the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The sum is text with an aria-label that says it: “6 times 4”. A new card, a result and the freeze are announced through Duel’s aria-live="assertive" region.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys.

Focus

It never takes focus; focus passes to the controls around it.

Labelling

The answer box is labelled “Your answer: 24”.

Motion

A new card flips in within 300 ms. A wrong answer shakes it once. With reduced motion both are a fade of 200 ms at most and nothing moves.

Touch

It is not a target, so it needs no target size.

Content

  • Right: “= 24 You got it!”.
  • Wrong: “Not 42! Frozen for a sec…”.
  • Lost: “{Name} said 24 first. So close!” and where the child now sits. No red screen, no buzzer.
  • Time up: “Time’s up! It was 24. Here comes a new card…”.
