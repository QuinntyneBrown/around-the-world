# Feature: Feel the hop with motion and sound

Journey 10 of `docs/journeys.md`. Hop and card flip animations, gentle losing, sounds, mute, reduced motion.

## Goal

The hop is the reward and lasts about a second; a new flash card flips in; a wrong answer
gives the card one short shake, as a refused code shakes the code's boxes on Room. Losing a desk
is gentle: no red screen, no buzzer. Every sound can be muted from Home or the settings dialog,
and the choice is kept on the device. Reduced motion replaces hops and card flips with quick
fades.

This journey builds on journeys 01 to 09 in `docs/journeys.md`. Before you start, check
their % complete there and in the code. If one this journey needs is not done, build the smallest
part of it this journey needs first, record that in your summary, and go on.
