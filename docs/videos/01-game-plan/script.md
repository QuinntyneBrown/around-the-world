# 01 · Around the World

## The classroom

Imagine your classroom as a world map. France is one desk, Egypt is another, and a tiny landmark tells you where you are. Your trip starts with one maths question.

## Your kid

Pick a kid. Zuri carries her pencil like a sword. Kenji collects pins, Nia loves to race, Milo brings his robot Bolt, and Pip brings a paper plane. Roll a nickname and you’re ready.

## Your room

Play solo against classmates on your device, or invite friends. The host reads a four-letter room code aloud. Up to eight players can join. While you watch a duel, you can cheer with one of six stickers.

## The tour

Choose six, twelve or twenty-four desks. Keep the default times tables, or practise addition, subtraction and division. Choose your speed, then press Go.

## The answer

The traveller stands beside the desk holder. When the card appears, shout the answer. Mic Check lets you practise first. You can always use the number pad instead.

## The duel

An incorrect answer freezes your pad for one second. The first correct answer wins the duel. Watch the stamp land when the traveller earns the next country.

## The walk

Follow Zuri from Egypt to Japan. Her feet take alternating steps along the dotted ring. If the desk holder answers first, they become the traveller and the other kid takes their seat.

## The passport

Make it all the way round to win. If time runs out, the player who travelled furthest wins. On the Podium, your passport shows the countries you reached. Ready for another tour?
